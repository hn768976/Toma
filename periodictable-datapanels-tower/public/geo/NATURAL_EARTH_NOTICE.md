# Natural Earth — 1:110m Land

`ne_110m_land.geojson` is the Natural Earth 1:110m physical "land" layer
(GeoJSON build from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector,
`geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson`).

Natural Earth data is in the **public domain**. No permission is needed to use
it, and no attribution is required (credit: "Made with Natural Earth" is
appreciated). See https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.

It is used by the Data Panels look, rasterised once at load time into an
equirectangular land mask.
