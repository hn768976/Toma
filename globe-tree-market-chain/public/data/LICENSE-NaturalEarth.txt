Natural Earth — Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com

ne_50m_land.json is derived from the Natural Earth 1:50m land dataset
(version 4.1.0) as redistributed in the "world-atlas" npm package
(TopoJSON, https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas), converted to plain
[lon, lat] polygons.
