Map data: Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) — public domain.
"Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com."

The files world-50m.json and world-110m.json were generated by
scripts/build-map-data.mjs from the TopoJSON conversion of Natural Earth
1:50m and 1:110m (land + admin-0 countries) published in the `world-atlas`
npm package (ISC licence, see world-atlas-LICENSE.txt). Coordinates are
rounded to 0.01 degrees.
