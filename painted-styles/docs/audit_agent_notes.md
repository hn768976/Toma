## artdeco — 38.7s @1080p
Best of group: clean 1930s poster lake, sunburst, faceted mountains, stepped pines, ripple-dash mirror. Issues: mountain shadow facets slab-like; sun hidden; reeds faint; speckle; lib has no landscape primitives (all built from poly/fade/spray/glow). Grain, ripple amplitude and finish() noise are in pixels (not resolution-independent). 10 stages.
## shadowpuppet — 50.4s
Charming amber lamp-lit screen with dyed carved leather; strips of carved holes busy, water mottled teal; blues go muddy (transmission), mist can only darken so it is weak; reflections = flipped soft pieces with no ripple; figure() too detailed at boat scale. Hide noise/weave/blur/bloom in pixels. 9 stages.
## xray — 34.4s
Convincing blue radiograph but flat and low-detail; additive (no occlusion) so sun removed; pines read as white spikes; skeleton/birds/reeds barely visible. Edge enhancement/glow/noise in pixels. Novelty only. 9 stages.
## vhs — 55.1s

## ballpoint — 46.4s
