# Natural Earth — 1:110m Land

`ne_110m_land.geojson` is the Natural Earth 1:110m physical "Land" layer
(GeoJSON build from github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector).

Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @ naturalearthdata.com.

Natural Earth data is in the **public domain**: "All versions of Natural Earth
raster + vector map data found on this website are in the public domain. You
may use the maps in any manner, including modifying the content and design,
electronic dissemination, and offset printing." (naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use)

It is used only by the Data Montage look: rasterised once into a land mask for
the dotted world maps and the dotted globes.
