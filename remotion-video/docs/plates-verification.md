## Loop seam

Mean absolute pixel difference across a downsampled frame. `step` is an
ordinary one-frame move (frame 0 to 1); `wrap` is the loop point (last
frame back to frame 0). A seamless loop makes the two comparable - a
visible seam shows up as a wrap value far above the step.

| Plate | step | wrap | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GoldenRays` | 1.09/255 | 1.38/255 | seamless |
| `SilverBokeh` | 0.99/255 | 0.96/255 | seamless |
| `DeepBlueDust` | 0.97/255 | 0.94/255 | seamless |
| `BokehCurtain` | 4.71/255 | 4.56/255 | seamless |
